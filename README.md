This entry erases the word "moist" from the dictionary forever. It is the most
universally disliked-sounding word in English: millions of people flinch at its
sound alone, so deleting it is a public service and the joke lands the instant
the word is gone, with no caption needed. The humour comes entirely from the
word itself — its damp, squelchy sound — and from the gap it leaves behind:
a language that no longer has to say it.
